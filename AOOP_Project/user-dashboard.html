About this campaign

A nationwide e-waste collection drive accepting laptops, phones, tablets, monitors, and small appliances. Every item is responsibly dismantled, with recovered materials sent to certified recyclers. Participants receive digital certificates tracking their environmental impact.

Rewards: 2x reward points on every accepted item, plus a free reusable tote for first 1,000 participants.

Who can join

Open to all individuals and small businesses. Bulk donors (10+ items) can request free home pickup.
